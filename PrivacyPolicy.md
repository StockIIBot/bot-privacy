Privacy Policy
1. What this is

This Privacy Policy explains what kind of data the bot collects, why it collects it, and how it’s used.

By using the bot, you agree to this policy. If you don’t agree, you shouldn’t use the bot.

2. What we store

To make the bot work properly, we store a small amount of data related to how you use it, such as:

Your Discord User ID
Server (Guild) ID
Your balance in the virtual economy
Your gambling results and transaction history
Stock holdings and trading history
Cooldowns (like daily rewards)
Basic command usage (mainly for debugging and preventing abuse)

We don’t collect anything personal like:

Your messages

Your password

Your email

Any private Discord data outside of what the bot needs
3. Why we collect it

We only store data so the bot can function properly. For example:

Saving your money and progress in the economy
Tracking stock trades and portfolio changes
Making sure commands like !daily, !pay, and !gamble work correctly
Preventing abuse or exploits in the system

Without storing this data, the bot wouldn’t be able to remember anything between sessions.

4. Sharing your data

We don’t sell, share, or give your data to anyone else.

Your data is only used by the bot itself to run its features.

5. How your data is stored

We store data securely using standard protection methods. Access to the database is restricted and only used for bot functionality and maintenance.

That said, no system is perfectly secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute protection.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data as long as it’s needed for the bot to function.

If the bot shuts down or data is no longer required, it may be deleted.

7. Deleting your data

If you want your data removed, you can use:

!deletedata

This will remove your economy data, stock information, and other stored details linked to your account (where possible).

8. Third-party services

The bot may use hosting services or databases to operate. These services only process data as needed to keep the bot running.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy might change from time to time. If you keep using the bot after changes, that means you accept the updated version.

10. Contact

If you have questions or want your data removed manually, you can contact the developer through the support server or direct Discord contact (if available).
